**April**  
Nordic spring weather is famously unpredictable, and the April motif in the 2027 Nature Calendar is a dramatic testament to exactly that. The image was captured during Hurricane Dave, a day when the North Sea truly unleashed its fury, with wind gusts in Hirtshals reaching a staggering 37.4 meters per second.

<!--more-->

Capturing this raw moment required more than just a good eye; it required constant vigilance. I was standing on the pier in Hirtshals, looking out over the harbor as the storm roared. My goal was to freeze the intense contrast between the dark, heavy water and the chaotic, white sea foam into one clean, graphic composition. However, massive waves kept crashing brutally over the concrete edges of the pier. I had to keep a constant watch on the incoming swells, dodging the heavy walls of water to protect myself and my fragile camera gear from the unforgiving saltwater.
