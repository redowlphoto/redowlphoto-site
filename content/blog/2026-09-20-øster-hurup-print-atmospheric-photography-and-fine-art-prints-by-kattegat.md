The beach at Øster Hurup and the coastline stretching down past Als Odde towards Kattegat is one of the most captivating natural areas in North Jutland. Here, the open sea, sand dunes, and a very special, gentle light meet and shift as the sun descends over the land.

<!--more-->

Late one afternoon by the water’s edge in Øster Hurup, I experienced how the beautiful, warm sunset light was cast far across the sea as the sun set inland. The sky and water blended together in soft, golden and muted hues, while the dunes framed the tranquil coastal landscape in a deep, meditative calm. It is precisely this unique evening atmosphere that makes the local coastline so fascinating to capture.
