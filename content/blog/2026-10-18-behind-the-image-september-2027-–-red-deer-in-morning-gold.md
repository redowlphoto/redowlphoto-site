**September**  
This motif was photographed in Lille Vildmose in North Jutland, located close to the holiday town of Øster Hurup and the beautiful beaches of the east coast. The nature type here consists of raised bogs and ancient forests, harboring a truly unique piece of wildlife history. While many red deer in the rest of Denmark descend from deer imported from Germany centuries ago to improve hunting trophies, the deer in Lille Vildmose are the very last true descendants of Denmark’s original, native red deer.

I love spending an hour out in Lille Vildmose just around sunrise. The light and wildlife ensure there is always something incredibly beautiful to witness. On this particular morning, the sun turned the dense fog into a glowing golden blanket just as this magnificent stag emerged.
