**November**  
This motif was photographed at Als Odde in Himmerland, where Denmark’s most beautiful fjord, Mariagerfjord, meets the open waters of the Kattegat sea on the east coast. The nature type here is a beautiful blend of protected fjord landscapes and coastal expanses.

During autumn and winter, the light in Denmark changes. Because the sun sits lower in the sky, the light often falls along the length of the fjord, reaching far out into the sea. This creates incredibly beautiful mornings and evenings, where the calm water reflects the soft colors of the sky. I personally love taking a drive down to the small boat mooring to simply enjoy the nature and let the tranquility settle in.

<!--more-->

This image is crafted to bring exactly that mood straight into your home. And while the photograph was taken at Mariagerfjord, the small boat and the mirror-like water represent a serene atmosphere that characterizes all the small and large fjords scattered across Denmark.

### Unobtrusive design and sustainable quality
When bringing such a peaceful motif indoors, the calendar's practical elements must not disrupt the experience. The month is placed at the bottom, with an elegant indication of Sundays and public holidays. The colors of the date area are carefully chosen to blend naturally with the gentle tones of the photograph, keeping the tranquility unbroken.

This work is crafted with great respect for nature. It is printed on **premium textured art paper** (**250 gsm**), which brings out the delicate nuances of the water's reflection. Moreover, it is an **eco-friendly fine art calendar printed on up to 50% recycled materials**, ensuring that it honors the environment it depicts.
