**February**  
We continue the new year with atmospheric calm. The February motif in my exclusive wall calendar for 2027 is a minimalist dive into the special silence that occurs when sea fog rolls in over the coast. 

The image was captured early one morning at Als Odde, where the fog drifted heavily over the Kattegat coast, dissolving the horizon into a cool, grey haze while the soft light reflected on the perfectly still sea.

<!--more-->

The goal with this particular motif was to bring the dreamy, quiet, and unadulterated coastal atmosphere indoors. What caught my eye that morning was how the dark wooden poles of the old pound nets broke the bright, reflecting water surface. 

By letting the sea fog remove all unnecessary background noise, the poles emerged almost like a charcoal drawing against the endless space of water and sky. This gave the image a strong graphic and minimalist expression, which is the very essence of Nordic fine art nature photography.

**Printed on exclusive art paper**
