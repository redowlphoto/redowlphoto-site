**May**  
Spring has truly arrived in the May motif of the 2027 Nature Calendar. The image was captured on a perfectly still, early morning in the vast meadows surrounding the Skjern Å river. A striking, crooked, and windblown tree stands as a solitary sculpture in the landscape, while the light morning mist slowly lifts over the flat terrain.

What fascinates me about this particular tree is its ability to tell a story about the harsh West Jutland weather, even on a calm spring morning. The goal was to capture the contrast between the dramatic shape of the tree and the soft, infinite peace of the meadow.
