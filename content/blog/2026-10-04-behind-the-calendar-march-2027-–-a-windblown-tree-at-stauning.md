**March**  
In the first spring month of the year, we head to the west coast of Jutland. The March motif was captured early one morning just before sunrise at Stauning, with Ringkøbing Fjord resting quietly in the background. The mist hung unusually thick over the landscape, and right there, this striking, windblown tree caught my full attention.
