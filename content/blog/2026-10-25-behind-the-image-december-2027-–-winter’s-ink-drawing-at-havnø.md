**December**  
The final motif of the year is one very close to my heart. This photograph was taken in the fields of Havnø Estate in North Jutland, close to Hadsund and near the beautiful Danish east coast. The nature type here consists of historic manor landscapes and ancient, crooked trees that stand as resilient monuments against the coastal winds.

This specific picture was captured early in the morning, right around sunrise, immediately following a severe snowstorm. The day before, fierce winds from the north had blasted the landscape, leaving the snow pressed tightly against the trunk and northern branches of this old oak tree. The result was an almost surreal scene: the contours of the tree stood out sharply, resembling a delicate, monochrome ink drawing, while light snow continued to fall.

<!--more-->

It was a fleeting, magical moment that will likely never be repeated in exactly the same way. This image, which has earned recognition in both national and international photography competitions, now hangs as a large format print in my own living room because I simply never tire of looking at it.

### Minimalist design on sustainable premium paper

The colors of the date area are carefully designed to blend naturally with the snowy tones of the photograph, ensuring that the visual experience of this unique "portrait" remains undisturbed.

This physical presentation respects the nature it portrays. It is printed on **premium textured art paper** (**250 gsm**), which beautifully accentuates the graphic lines of the snowy branches.
