Als Odde is one of the most atmospheric spots along the east coast, situated right where Mariager Fjord flows into the Kattegat sea — just a short drive from the seaside resort town of Øster Hurup. It is a scenic nature area where you can often spot grey herons along the shore, seals resting by the water, or grab a great burger right by the waterfront at the cozy local Hillbilly Grill.

<!--more-->

On a still April evening, I stood at the water's edge at Als Odde. The fjord was like a mirror, and as dusk fell, it left behind a soft, diffused light in delicate blue and pink shades. The reflections give the scene a dreamy quality, where sea and sky merge seamlessly.
