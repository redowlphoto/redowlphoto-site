**July**  
This motif was photographed at Rubjerg Knude Lighthouse near Lønstrup in Vendsyssel, North Jutland. The nature type in this area consists of dramatic coastal cliffs and massive shifting sand dunes meeting the North Sea. It is one of my absolute favorite locations in Denmark, as the lighthouse's freestanding position on the sand offers endless opportunities for spectacular fine art photography.

However, this specific photograph holds a story that reaches far beyond the Danish borders.

<!--more-->

The image was taken on a late afternoon, just around sunset. The light over the sea should have been a standard golden hue, but instead, it turned into an intense, almost unnaturally yellow color. The reason was the massive wildfires raging in both Spain and Canada at the time. Immense amounts of smoke had drifted into the atmosphere, acting as a giant filter for the sun's rays, even here on the Jutland west coast. The result is a stunningly beautiful scenery, yet layered with sadness for the nature and people affected by the fires.

### A design that respects the story
When a motif carries such a profound atmosphere, the calendar's design should not interfere.
