**June**  
When the wind finally drops and the air turns mild and warm across Denmark, truly magical moments arise along the coasts. The June motif in the 2027 Nature Calendar is a tribute to this rare, indescribable summer tranquility.

This motif was photographed at Als Odde in North Jutland, where the coastline of Mariager Fjord meets the open waters of the Kattegat sea. The nature type in this area usually ranges from raw and windswept to infinitely beautiful and quiet. This picture captures an evening of the latter kind, where the pastel light is cast softly along the fjord, creating a mirror-like canvas for the small, anchored boat.

<!--more-->

The hope with this photograph is to let the warm colors and mild atmosphere of the summer evening reach right through the paper. However, this requires a presentation free from visual noise.

### Fine art photography without distractions
To preserve the aesthetic calm, I have kept the calendar's design incredibly minimalist. The calendar displays the month at the bottom, highlighting Sundays and public holidays elegantly and subtly. The colors of the date area are carefully designed so that they blend naturally with the photo of the month. This ensures that you can absorb the colors and mood from the fjord without the actual calendar function ever disturbing the experience.
