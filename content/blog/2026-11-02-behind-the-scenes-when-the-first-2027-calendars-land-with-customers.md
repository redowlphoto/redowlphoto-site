**The joy of returning customers and the first orders**  
One of the most rewarding aspects of working with nature photography is the direct connection with the people who choose to hang my images on their walls. This week, I had the great pleasure of shipping out the very first copies of the 2027 Nature Calendar – filled with motifs from Mariager Fjord and the Jutland coastlines – to this year's earliest buyers.

<!--more-->

One of the very first orders to arrive came from Tove, who also supported my work last year. Hearing her explain that the 2026 edition brought so much joy to her everyday life that she has now chosen to order two new copies for the coming year means the world to me as a photographer. That sense of lasting joy is exactly what I hope to create with my images.

### Sustainable nature for your wall
When you order an **Eksklusiv kalender 2027**, the goal is for the quality to reflect the very nature the images portray. The calendar is crafted in a practical format of 30.5 x 20.3 cm (corresponding to a classic A4 size), making it easy to find a spot for it. It is printed on **premium textured art paper** (**250 gsm**), which beautifully brings out the misty mornings of Jutland. Furthermore, it is an **eco-friendly fine art calendar printed on up to 50% recycled materials**. Whether you buy it for yourself or give it away as a stunning **Fotokalender Nordjylland**, you bring a piece of uncompromising Nordic nature into your home.

### Secure ordering and safe delivery
To make the purchasing experience even better, I have updated the payment system on my website this year. Tove was the first to test the new flow, and her feedback was perfectly clear: the ordering process went completely without a hitch.

To ensure your fine art arrives flawlessly, the calendar is always packed and shipped securely in a thick, rigid cardboard envelope clearly marked "Do not bend". This protects the heavy art paper throughout the transit. Tove received her calendars a few days after ordering and could track her package safely the entire way via the included Track & Trace.
