Øster Hurup is one of the most atmospheric places along the east coast, situated right on the Kattegat sea – just a few minutes' drive from Als Odde and Mariager Fjord. It is a scenic area where, during summer, you can enjoy the vibrant beach life, take long walks along the calm waters, or experience the unique coastal light.

<!--more-->

Early one morning, I stood at the water's edge on the beach in Øster Hurup, waiting for the sunrise over the sea. Just as the sun was about to break free above the horizon, a dense sea fog drifted in from the north over the Kattegat. The sun was completely veiled by the fog, leaving only its warm light rays to pierce through. It truly highlights how changeable the weather can be right by the sea, and why Øster Hurup and the coastline stretching past Als to Als Odde never look quite the same.
