## The Story Behind the Image

It was a calm April evening. The fjord water was glassy and undisturbed, and the sun had just dipped below the horizon. All that remained was a soft glow in pastel blue and pink hues.

Because the wooden jetties had not yet been put out for the summer season, only the bare posts stood straight in the water. I have photographed these posts at Als Odde many times before, but on this particular evening, everything aligned. The sky and water melted together, and the reflections created a dreamy, almost weightless version of reality.

## Visiting Notes for Als Odde

- **Location:** Als Odde at the mouth of **Mariager Fjord**, near **Øster Hurup** and **Aalborg**.
- **Best Times:** Immediately after sunset on calm evenings, or at first light at dawn when the water is motionless.
- **Take Note:** Tidal currents where the fjord meets the sea change the sandbanks and water reflections from hour to hour.

*I always return to Als Odde. It is one of those places along the fjord where you feel the calm rhythm of nature most clearly.*
