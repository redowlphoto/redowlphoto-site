**August**  
This motif was photographed early in the morning at Råbjerg Mile near Skagen in North Jutland. The nature type here is a massive, desert-like migrating sand dune containing roughly 3.5 million cubic meters of sand. Driven by the strong westerly winds, the dune constantly moves eastward, eventually destined to disappear into the sea.

If you arrive early in the morning and step away from the main paths, you are met with a profound feeling of being enveloped in absolute nothingness. With no vegetation anywhere, the landscape feels incredibly barren, vast, and solitary.

<!--more-->

It was exactly this overwhelming emptiness and scale I wanted to capture for the August motif. If you look closely into the vast sandscape, you can actually spot the photographer himself, perfectly illustrating the immense scale of this natural phenomenon.
