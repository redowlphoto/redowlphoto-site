The work on next year's calendar is finally complete. The 2027 calendar is a curated collection of twelve landscapes, tracing the shifting light, changing seasons, and quiet atmospheres across some of my absolute favorite locations in Jutland.

<!--more-->

The heart of this year's calendar is the desire to capture the raw, unpolished moods of nature and bring them indoors. The visual journey through the twelve months spans a wide range of atmospheres—from the soft, diffused morning mist in Skjern Enge to the dramatic, heavy skies during a storm in Hirtshals. You will find the crisp, biting light of winter frost, the peaceful pastel evenings by Mariager Fjord, the golden autumn hues of Lille Vildmose, and the vast, sun-bleached expanses of Råbjerg Mile.

**Printed on Exclusive Fine Art Paper**  
To truly capture these subtle shifts in light and give the images a beautiful, organic feel all year round, the calendar is printed on heavy, premium fine art paper. Produced at a professional photo lab, this ensures deep, rich tones and true-to-life colors without any distracting glare.

**A Perfect Gift for the Outdoorsman**  
Do you know someone who feels most at home under the open sky—perhaps a hunter, angler, hiker, or sailor? The calendar is a thoughtful gift for anyone who appreciates the changing seasons and the raw beauty of the Nordic outdoors. 

For that reason, I have added a special price when you order two copies: Give one away to a fellow nature lover, and keep the other for yourself.
