**October**  
This motif was photographed in the fields surrounding Als and Hadsund in North Jutland, close to the beautiful Danish east coast. The nature type here consists of open farmland broken up by distinct lines of forest. October is the time when nature shuts down for winter. Viewed from inside a warm living room, the dark, cold afternoons often seem uninviting. But once you actually get out there, the dark months offer incredibly magical moments. 

This picture was taken on a perfectly still evening just after sunset, as the light faded and bathed the world in an intense, almost purplish-blue tone. A single tree stands solitary, yet embraced by the surrounding landscape.

<!--more-->

For me, experiencing this light is one of the greatest joys of photography: it gives you a reason to get out there. It is a feeling deeply understood by dog walkers, hunters, horseback riders, and anyone who works outdoors year-round. We know the light is out there, waiting for those who seek it.
