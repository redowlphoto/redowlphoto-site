The lunar eclipse in September 2025 was one of those nights where everything had to align. I stood on the coast at Als, near the popular holiday town of Øster Hurup and east of **Mariagerfjord**, camera ready. The air was damp, and low clouds hung over the sea.

<!--more-->

At first, the moon was hidden behind a thick layer of low clouds, looking like one of those nights where nature simply shuts you out. But then something happened. Slowly, the moon broke through the veil — soft, muted, and almost surreal in its light.

In that moment, it no longer felt like a distant celestial body, but like something newly born, emerging from the darkness and mist. That is where the image found its name: **Newborn Moon**.

- **Atmospheric Nordic stillness** – captures the rare moment where cosmic drama meets coastal quiet.
- **Award-winning astrophotography** – awarded **Global Photographer of the Year** at the Global Photography Awards 2025.
